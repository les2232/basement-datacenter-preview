Privacy in Mom, It's a Startup

The game itself collects no personal data. Progress and settings are saved on this device. You choose whether to send a backup code or copy game details into feedback. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. We do not share player data. Use “Reset game” to erase your progress.

The landing page has no ads, analytics or tracking. GitHub Pages hosts both the landing page and the game. When you visit either one online, GitHub Pages automatically logs your IP address to keep the hosting service secure and operating reliably. GitHub processes these logs as our hosting service provider. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

For a privacy question, open an issue on the game’s support page. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
